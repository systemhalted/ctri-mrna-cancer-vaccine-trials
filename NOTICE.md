# Notice

This repository contains clinical-trial research, public-registry metadata, and software for identifying potential therapeutic mRNA cancer-vaccine studies relevant to India.

## Research snapshot and verification

Clinical-trial information changes frequently. Recruitment status, study locations, eligibility criteria, investigator contacts, and sponsor information in this repository are research snapshots as of their stated retrieval or analysis dates and may become outdated.

Always verify any study against the live official registry and the study sponsor or participating site before relying on it for patient decisions.

## Registry attribution and data use

ClinicalTrials.gov and CTRI are primary source registries used in this research. Where registry data are reproduced or summarized, the repository should retain source links and snapshot dates. The project does not claim ownership of underlying registry records.

Published investigator and trial-coordinator contact information is included only for clinical-trial research and potential patient-enrollment inquiries. It must not be used for marketing, bulk solicitation, spam, or unrelated promotional activity.

## CTRI access controls

CTRI Advanced Search requires a human-entered Security Code/CAPTCHA. This project does not automate, solve, bypass, defeat, or otherwise circumvent that control, authentication mechanisms, rate limits, or other access restrictions.

## No patient data

Do not commit patient-identifiable information or medical records to this repository. This includes names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, government or insurance identifiers, pathology reports, imaging files, genomic files tied to a person, treatment records, or other protected/sensitive health information.

If a trial team requests records for screening, use the verified secure transfer mechanism provided by the investigator, hospital, or sponsor.

## Medical-use limitation

This repository is a research aid, not medical advice and not an eligibility determination. Trial eligibility, enrollment, treatment decisions, and medical suitability must be determined by qualified clinicians and the relevant trial investigators.

## Copyright and third-party content

Prefer summaries, factual extraction, and links to original registry or sponsor sources over copying substantial third-party text. Third-party material remains subject to its applicable copyright, database, and usage terms.
